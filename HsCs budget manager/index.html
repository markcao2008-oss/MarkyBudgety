📖 How to Use MarkyBudgety / 使用指南

▼

🇬🇧 Welcome to MarkyBudgety!

This workspace is designed to keep your personal finances completely structured. Here is how it operates:

  • Smart Cashflow: Use the form to record incoming funds or outgoing expenses. Choose custom categories to automatically generate dynamic monthly pie graphs and charts.
  • Sequential Wishlist Goals: Put down targets you are aiming to buy. The app automatically allocates your surplus current wallet balance downward through your wishlist hierarchy! Once a item reaches 100%, hit Purchase to permanently deduct it and log it instantly.
  • Debt Controls: Track funds you owe to others. Pay items back gradually using inline inputs, which handles wallet reductions seamlessly.
  • External Vault Storage: Track assets kept elsewhere (like separate banking accounts, crypto vaults, or cash boxes). Type in a number and click Withdraw to transfer those reserves instantly back straight into your main wallet balance!

🇨🇳 欢迎使用 MarkyBudgety！

这是一个专为追踪个人财务打造的智能面板。系统核心功能如下：

  • 智能现金流： 记录你的每一笔收入与支出，选择对应标签（如运动训练、娱乐等）系统将全自动生成月度财务分析图表。
  • 阶梯式心愿单： 输入你想购买的目标。系统会根据你当前的钱包余额，**由上至下**自动分配闲置资金！当进度条达到 100% 时，点击 Purchase (购买)，系统会自动扣款并将其记入历史日志。
  • 债务管理： 清晰追踪你欠别人的资金。你可以直接在卡片中输入金额并点击扣除，钱包余额会同步智能更新。
  • 外部资产视窗： 专门记录存放在其他地方的备用资金（如父母代管、独立账户或现金）。需要使用时，输入金额点击 Withdraw (提现)，该笔资金便会瞬间注入并增加到你的钱包可用余额中！
